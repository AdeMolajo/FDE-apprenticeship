## S09. Convert dates to ISO dates

**Rule:** Excel date cells are used as-is. Text is parsed by exact pattern: yyyy-mm-dd; dd/mm/yyyy (read as UK, since the ledger is a UK GBP ledger); d-Mon-yy. When a dd/mm date is also a valid mm/dd date, check both readings against invoice-number order: if only the UK reading keeps dates in sequence it is confirmed, otherwise it is flagged.

**Why:** Three date formats in one column, and dd/mm vs mm/dd cannot be told apart by pattern alone.

- Formats found: ISO yyyy-mm-dd text: 10; dd/mm/yyyy text (UK): 7; Excel date cell: 8.
